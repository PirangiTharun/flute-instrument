Bansuri lessons – C natural, medium flute

S

Play connects the notes legato in one continuous breath. Right-handed view: you look down at your own hands. Mouth hole on the right, right hand on holes 1–3, left hand on holes 4–6. Dark hole = closed, light = open, half-dark = half hole. Tap any note to jump to it. Fingerings follow the PDF chart (middle octave); check them against your teacher’s, and edit the F table in the page source if needed.
